Disease Prediction using Machine Learning

An in-progress classification project focused on careful data preparation, model comparison and appropriately cautious interpretation.

  • StatusIn Progress
  • DomainHealth data
  • FocusClassification
  • Primary languagePython

01

Overview

The project is being developed as an evidence-led classification workflow rather than a claim of clinical readiness.

The intended work covers cleaning structured data, understanding class balance and feature quality, comparing suitable baseline models and reporting classification metrics with clear limitations. Results will be added only after the experimental record is complete.

06

Challenges

Data quality

Missing values, inconsistent records and class imbalance can distort conclusions.

Leakage prevention

Transformations must be learned from training data without exposing held-out outcomes.

Metric selection

Accuracy alone may be inadequate when the costs of errors differ.

Responsible scope

An academic classifier must not be presented as a diagnostic system.

07

What I Learned

  • How early data-quality decisions shape downstream model reliability.
  • Why preprocessing belongs inside a reproducible evaluation pipeline.
  • How to choose metrics in relation to the practical meaning of errors.
  • Why health-related predictions require explicit boundaries and cautious communication.
