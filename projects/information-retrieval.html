Information Retrieval

A search-ranking project exploring BM25 scoring, text preprocessing and the mechanics of matching queries to relevant documents.

  • StatusComplete
  • ContextUniversity work
  • MethodBM25
  • Primary languagePython

01

Overview

The project examines how a retrieval system turns a natural-language query into a ranked list of documents.

BM25 provides the core ranking function, balancing term frequency, inverse document frequency and document-length normalisation. The work focuses on building the retrieval pipeline clearly and interpreting why particular documents receive higher scores.

Challenges

Text consistency

Applying the same tokenisation and normalisation decisions to documents and queries.

Ranking behaviour

Understanding the interaction between term frequency, rarity and document length.

Edge cases

Handling unmatched query terms and documents with limited useful content.

Responsible claims

Avoiding effectiveness statistics until a documented evaluation set is available.

07

What I Learned

  • How corpus-level statistics influence relevance ranking.
  • Why preprocessing choices are part of the retrieval model, not a separate detail.
  • How to structure a search pipeline into testable stages.
  • Why retrieval quality requires explicit relevance judgements and evaluation metrics.
